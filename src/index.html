    Nisar Office Studio

    Version 1.1 ·A free, private word processor. Your documents stay on this PC. There is no account, no tracking, and no internet needed.

    Opens and saves Word documents (.docx), web pages (.html) and plain text (.txt). Exports PDF. Formatting is kept exactly when a document is saved and reopened in Nisar Office Studio. Documents written in Microsoft Word or other apps open with their fonts, sizes, colours, highlights, alignment, spacing, lists, tables, pictures, links, page setup, headers and footers. Text boxes, comments, tracked changes and footnotes are simplified.

    Unsaved work is backed up every 30 seconds. If the app closes unexpectedly, you'll be offered your document the next time it starts.
