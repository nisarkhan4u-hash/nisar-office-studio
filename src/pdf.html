    Nisar Office Studio · PDF

    View, fill in, sign, comment on, edit and reorganise PDF files. Everything stays on this PC.

    Comments, drawings, text, pictures and signatures you add are written into the PDF when you save, so they show up in every PDF reader. Nisar Office Studio also keeps a private copy of your edits inside the file, so when you reopen it here you can still move, change or delete them.

    Whiteout covers content with white, but the text underneath can still be copied out of the file. Redact removes it for good: when you save, redacted pages are turned into images so the hidden text no longer exists.
