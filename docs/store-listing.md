## Description (up to 10,000 characters; this is about 1,700)

> Nisar Office Studio is a free, open-source word processor and PDF editor that keeps your files private. It works completely offline: there is no account, no advertising, no tracking, and nothing you write is uploaded anywhere.
>
> **Documents**
> - Write and format documents with fonts, colours, styles, headings, lists, tables, pictures, links, page breaks, headers and footers, and page numbers.
> - Open and save .docx documents, as well as web pages (.html) and plain text (.txt).
> - Page setup: paper size, orientation, margins and columns, with a page view that shows where pages break.
> - Comments, tracked changes, table of contents, footnotes, navigation pane and watermarks.
> - Find and replace, spelling check, word count, dark mode.
> - Export a document to PDF and print.
>
> **PDFs**
> - View PDFs, search their text and jump between pages and bookmarks.
> - Fill in forms or create your own form fields; add text, pictures, stamps, drawings, highlights and sticky-note comments.
> - Sign with a drawn or typed signature, and keep signatures for next time.
> - Rearrange, rotate, delete, crop, insert and extract pages; combine several PDFs, turn pictures into a PDF, or split one into parts.
> - Headers, footers, page numbers, Bates numbers and watermarks.
> - Redact: remove content permanently, or find and redact every match of a word or number.
> - Password-protect PDFs with AES-256 encryption, or remove a password you know.
> - Recognize text (OCR) in scanned pages so they become searchable and selectable.
> - Reduce file size and remove hidden information.
> - Convert a PDF into an editable document.
>
> **Private by design**
> Your files stay on your computer. The app collects no personal data and works without an internet connection.
>
> **Open source**
> Nisar Office Studio is free software under the MIT licence. The source code is on GitHub.

## Trademark rules

Do not use "Microsoft Word", "Microsoft Office", "Adobe" or "Acrobat" in the name, description, search terms or screenshots. (The plain word "Office" in the app's name is fine; WPS Office, ONLYOFFICE and LibreOffice are listed in the Store the same way.) Describing file formats (".docx", "PDF") is fine. Store certification rejects listings that suggest a link to another company's product.
