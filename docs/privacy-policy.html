Privacy Policy

Nisar Office Studio for Windows · Effective date: 20 October 2026

In short: Nisar Office Studio works offline. It does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your documents stay on your computer.

What the app does with your files

  • Documents and PDFs you open, create or edit are processed only on your computer, inside the app.
  • Files are saved only in the folder you choose.
  • Text recognition (OCR), PDF editing, PDF passwords, Word (.docx) conversion and printing all run on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Settings (such as dark mode, your name for comments, recent files and saved signatures) and an automatic recovery copy of unsaved work are stored only on your computer, in your Windows user profile.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking code, and it does not send your documents, settings or any information about you or your computer to the developer or to anyone else.

Internet use

The app does not need an internet connection. It only goes online when you ask it to, for example when you click a link in a document (which opens in your web browser or mail app). If a document you open contains pictures that are linked from a web address rather than stored in the file, Windows may download those pictures, the same way a web browser would.

Microsoft Store

If you install the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, updates and any reports you choose to send through Windows. That is covered by Microsoft's own privacy statement, not by this policy.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Open source

Nisar Office Studio is open-source software under the MIT licence, so anyone can check what it does by reading its source code.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
